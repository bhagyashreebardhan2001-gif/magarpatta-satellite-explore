How to use the map

A quick reference — beginner friendly, no GIS background needed.

🔍

Zoom & pan

Scroll to zoom, click and drag to pan around the study area.

📅

Select dates

Enter a start and end date to define the observation period.

☁

Cloud percentage

Set the maximum cloud cover allowed when selecting imagery.

🗂

Choose a layer

Switch between Sentinel-2, Landsat 9, and MODIS NDVI layers.

🌿

Reading NDVI colours

Brown → yellow → green shows sparse to dense, healthy vegetation.

📊

Charts & statistics

The NDVI time-series chart and image counts update automatically after you click "Update map."
